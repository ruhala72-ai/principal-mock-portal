CCS (CCA) Rule 10 90-Day Strict Window

Suspension & Review Mandate

Practical School Case Story: Principal ne anushasanheen shikshak ko suspend kiya. Prabandhan 90 din tak review meeting bulana bhool gaya. 91ve din shikshak ne High Court me challenge kiya jahan Rule 10(6) ke tahat suspension automatically lapse ghoshit kar diya gaya.
  • Nirnay: Suspension order 90 din ke baad bina review ke avaidh hai.
  • Extension: Ek baar me adhiktam 180 din tak badhaya ja sakta hai.
GFR 2017 & GeM GeM Slabs

Rule 149 & 157 (Anti-Splitting)

Audit Case Study: Vidyalaya ko computer lab ke liye ₹2,00,000 ka grant mila. Higher sanction aur GeM L-1 comparison se bachne ke liye bill ko ₹24,000 ke 8 tukdon me toda gaya. Audit me ise Rule 157 (Splitting of Demand) ka ullanghan mankar inquiry baitha di gayi.
  • Up to ₹25,000: Direct Purchase via GeM.
  • ₹25,001 to ₹5,00,000: GeM L-1 comparison (min 3 manufacturers).
  • Above ₹5,00,000: Mandatory online bidding / Reverse Auction.
UP Act 1921 Sec 16G 60-Day DIOS Rule

Conditions of Service & Suspension

Real School Case Story: Aided Inter College ke shikshak ko Prabandh Samiti ne suspend kiya. DIOS ne 60 din tak koi likhit aadesh nahi diya. Allahabad High Court ne Section 16G(7) ke anuroop tay kiya ki 60 din poore hote hi suspension automatic lapse ho gaya.
  • DIOS approval compulsory hai.
  • Regulation 10: 1 varsh ka probation period (extendable by max 1 year).
POCSO & POSH Acts Mandatory Reporting

Section 19 (POCSO) & ICC (POSH)

Institutional Safety Case: Principal ko vidyarthi ke sath abhadra vyavhar ki suchna mili. Unhone police ko suchit kiye bina aantarik committee se jaanch karwayi. Court ne POCSO Section 21 ke tahat Principal par reporting me deri ke arop me karyavahi ka aadesh diya.
  • POCSO Sec 19: Police/SJPU ko turant suchna dena anivarya.
  • POSH Sec 4: 10+ employees wale sansthan me ICC ka gathan zaroori (90-day inquiry).
NEP 2020 & UP Schemes 5+3+3+4 & Kayakalp

Structural Reform & Infrastructure

School Rejuvenation Scenario: Vidyalaya me enrollment girne par Principal ne 'Operation Kayakalp' ke 19 parameters aur 'Project ALANKAR' ke anudan se lab aur campus ka renovation karwaya aur FLN (NIPUN Bharat) me utkrisht parinaam diye.
  • NEP 2020: 5 (Foundational) + 3 (Prep) + 3 (Middle) + 4 (Secondary).
