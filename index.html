CCS (Conduct) Rules 1964 & CCS (CCA) Rules 1965

Rule 11: Minor Penalties (लघु शास्तियाँ)
  • (i) Censure (परिनिन्दा).
  • (ii) Withholding of promotion.
  • (iii) Recovery of pecuniary loss caused to Government.
  • (iv) Withholding of increments without cumulative effect (max 3 yrs).
Rule 11: Major Penalties (दीर्घ शास्तियाँ)
  • Reduction to lower stage in time-scale / lower grade.
  • Compulsory retirement.
  • Removal from service (not disqualifying for future govt jobs).
  • Dismissal from service (disqualifies for future govt jobs).
Rule 10: Suspension & 90-Day Review Rule

An order of suspension made under Rule 10 shall not be valid after 90 days unless it is extended after review by the competent review committee before the expiry of 90 days. Subsequent extensions cannot exceed 180 days at a time.

2

General Financial Rules (GFR 2017) & GeM Procurement

Rule 149 (GeM Procurement Slabs):
  • Up to ₹25,000: Direct purchase through any seller meeting quality/specifications.
  • ₹25,001 to ₹5,00,000: Purchase through seller having lowest price (L-1) amongst at least 3 different manufacturers.
  • Above ₹5,00,000: Mandatory online bidding / reverse auction on GeM portal.
Rule 157 (Anti-Splitting of Demands):

A demand for goods shall not be divided into small quantities to make piecemeal purchases to avoid obtaining higher financial sanctions or conducting open bids.

3

U.P. Intermediate Education Act 1921 & Commission Act 1982

Section 16G (Conditions of Service & Suspension Lapse)

No Principal, Headmaster or teacher can be suspended without the prior approval of the District Inspector of Schools (DIOS) in writing. An order of suspension passed by the Management Committee lapses automatically after 60 days unless approved in writing by the DIOS.

Regulation 10 (Probation Period)

Every teacher and Principal shall on substantive appointment be placed on probation for a period of one year, which may be extended by the Committee of Management by not more than one year with prior approval of the Inspector.
